Chapter 1: The Worldwide System (The Anatomy of Babylon)

By Raphael E. Asanti

Core Objective

To define "Babylon" not as an ancient Mesopotamian kingdom or a single geographic point on a map, but as a borderless, globalized system of polytheism, self-deification, and structural rebellion against the Creator.

The Prophetic Blueprint

"But the rest of mankind... did not repent of the works of their hands, that they should not worship demons, and idols of gold, silver, brass, stone, and wood..." — Revelation 9:20

To understand the core identity of Babylon, we must analyze the Koine Greek terms that define its spiritual posture:

  • Τὰ ἔργα τῶν χειρῶν (Ta erga tōn cheirōn): "the works of their hands." Babylon is a civilization that worships what it manufactures.
  • Ἀνομία (Anomia): lawlessness — Babylon functions in direct opposition to God's law.
