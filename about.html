About The Author

My name is Raphael E. Asanti and I'm a Sovereign Servant of the Lord God Almighty. I know some people think that's an outlandish statement of Authority, I view it as a responsibility. Angel means 'Messenger.' I do not relay these messages for personal enjoyment or ambition; I relay them out of absolute, fearful obedience combined with love, awe and respect for the One who sent me. The burden of the word is simply my duty.

There are seven angels written in the book of Revelation, each of us have our own church. A church in this regard is not a building or a specific locale, each of the churches are groups of people under an 'Angel' as their leader. My church is the church of Philadelphia. There are no members in my church - I'm a community of "Only Me'. The other angels of the other churches are also busy in the world these days with their own people. Think of them like factions - each has their own responsibilities, their own abilities, their own failings and their own agenda.

The other factions aren't happy with our eternal separation, but God Almighty enforces the Mizpah God Almighty decreed with a corpse hanging from a bridge on Mizpah Church Rd back in February 2023.

About Armageddon

Armageddon is a spiritual war, not a physical ground conflict. Each of the other 'Angels' of Revelation have their own various factions fighting this war. It's a spiritual war - a war for spiritual domination so that the 'winner' can put forth their own perverted version of what they believe the Kingdom of God should be like. They make alliances, they break alliances, they strategize, they plot, they maneuver and they act.

My pacifism is not a lack of agency; it is an act of absolute surrender to the Sovereign King whom God Almighty made to be slightly higher than the angels. I do not fight in your wars because the battle is not mine.

About Lazarus

Some of them know me as "Lazarus". Lazarus simply means "God has helped" (past tense) or "God is my help" (current tense). It means both. Jesus had a prophetic event regarding Lazarus that resulted in Lazarus being brought out of Bethany (Bethany means 'affliction') and sat at the table next to him reclining. This was a prophetic event symbolizing what would later come to be known as 'The Wedding Supper of the Lamb'. Reclining was symbolic of taking a day of rest vis a vis the final 1,000 year Sabbath.

The 'Alternate Ending' That Defined the Ultimatum

Jesus told another story of Lazarus. This one involved a rich man dressed in purple with Lazarus dying as a beggar - having only received 'bad' things. Lazarus is taken to Paradise, a barrier is put in place so the rich man and his brothers cannot go and the rich man is left without water to wet his tongue. Spoiler alert - the 'rich man' is the angel of the church of Laodicea and his five brothers are the angels of the other 5 churches of Revelation.
