Global Warming and the Destruction of Babylon

Index Page

This page provides a clear overview of the chapter exploring how global warming, tipping points, and the collapse of Earth’s systems connect to the imagery of Babylon in Revelation 18:21.

1. Babylon as a World System

Babylon is described in Revelation as a worldwide pattern of commerce, greed, and idolatry. It represents a global civilization that believes it cannot fall because it is wealthy, connected, and powerful. The modern world mirrors this pattern almost exactly, operating as a global system built on consumption, extraction, and distraction.

2. What Global Warming Actually Is

Global warming is not simply hotter weather. It is the breakdown of the planet’s entire life‑support system — the ecosystem and climate engine that make civilization possible. It affects air, oceans, forests, storms, seasons, ice, soil, plants, and animals. When one part breaks, the others begin breaking too.

3. The World Ecosystem

The world ecosystem is a single interconnected network. Forests create rain, oceans absorb heat, ice reflects sunlight, soil stores carbon, rivers carry nutrients, and seasons guide growth. When a major component collapses, the shock spreads through the entire system, reducing stability and resilience.

4. The Climate System

The climate system is the machinery that keeps the planet stable. Ocean currents move heat, jet streams guide weather, monsoons deliver rainfall, storm tracks shift energy, and temperature zones determine where crops can grow. Civilization depends on this stability. Without it, farming fails, cities flood, and water systems collapse.

5. How Far Gone the System Already Is

Many parts of the world system are already past their limits. Rainforests are drying, coral reefs are dying, permafrost is melting, ice sheets are cracking, ocean currents are weakening, storms are becoming erratic, humidity is rising, seasons are shifting, and ecosystems are collapsing quietly even when they still appear green.

6. Tipping Points and Cascades

A tipping point is when a system becomes so stressed that it changes suddenly and permanently. Once crossed, it cannot return to its previous state. When one tipping point fails, it pushes others forward, creating a cascade. The Amazon, Congo, and Southeast Asian forests, coral reefs, permafrost, Greenland, WAIS, and the AMOC are all approaching or crossing their tipping points.

7. What Happens If the Ice Falls Into the Sea

If the EAIS, WAIS, Greenland, and global glaciers collapse into the ocean, sea levels rise by tens of meters. Coastlines drown, cities vanish, islands disappear, ocean currents break down, weather becomes chaotic, food systems fail, governments destabilize, and mass migration becomes unavoidable. Civilization as we know it cannot survive this combination.

8. “Every Island Fled Away” — Revelation 16:20

John described the effect: islands disappearing. Today we understand the cause: massive sea‑level rise from collapsing ice sheets. When tens of meters of water enter the ocean, islands do not sink slowly — they vanish. The world map changes permanently, exactly as Revelation describes.

9. Babylon and the Modern World

Revelation’s Babylon is a global system built on commerce, greed, and idolatry. It collapses suddenly and cannot be rebuilt. The modern world is built on consumption, extraction, and denial. It is crossing tipping points and cannot return to stability. The symbolism and the physical reality align.

10. The Millstone and the Glacier

John saw a massive object thrown into the sea, symbolizing the fall of a world system. Today, we see massive glaciers collapsing into the ocean, destabilizing the planet and ending the global system built on excess. The ancient metaphor and the modern mechanism describe the same event in different language.

11. Final Takeaway

Global warming is not just hotter weather. It is the collapse of the world system — the ecosystem, the climate engine, and the civilization built on them. Revelation describes Babylon falling. Global warming shows how it falls. The “millstone” is the glacier. The “sea” is the destabilized ocean. The “fall of Babylon” is the collapse of the global system built on commerce, greed, and idolatry.
